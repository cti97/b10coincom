## 1. Verdict

**The consensus core of M5 is sound.** Committee derivation is a pure, totally ordered function of committed state; each epoch's committee is memoised once from the state at the deciding height and rebuilt identically on replay; every reader (engine config, router membership, tally, certificate gate on serve, record and adopt, HELLO and sync authorisation) takes the committee for the block's own height from that one source; the power cap converges to a fixed point that both packages validate identically; lock and round persistence are untouched by rotation. **Two conflicting blocks cannot commit at one height across a rotation.** The staking transitions themselves are correct: every validation precedes the first write, every sum is overflow-checked, the cooldown arithmetic is right at both boundaries, and all three types are chain-bound and nonce-consuming. The two residuals from the last report that were in scope here (tally duplicate check before verification, sync replay check before the rate bucket, the store format marker) are fixed and pinned.

**The problems are in the economics and the edges**, and three of them are serious enough to block a public run of this branch:

| Sev | ID | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | ST-1 | **A partial unbond keeps a sub-minimum bond as a committee candidate**, and the fixed-point cap then lifts a one-spark seat to equal power with any honest stake. One `MIN_STAKE` can be recycled into unlimited one-spark seats; three of them outvote everything. |
| **High** | RT-1 | **A validator that bonds after genesis cannot start a node.** The node and CLI refuse any key not in the genesis list, so rotation-in is unimplemented at the entry point: a bonded outsider's seat has no signer, and every such seat silently raises the quorum bar. |
| **High** | RT-2 | **On the shipped committee-file chain, genesis seats are bonded at one spark, `MIN_STAKE` is 10^11, and there are no spare seats.** One `MIN_STAKE` bond evicts a genesis seat; on the three-Pi recipe (1/1 cap) one bond holds about 100% of the power. The deploy README's funding arithmetic is off by two orders of magnitude. |
| **High** | PX-1 | **Peer-exchange hints are unsigned and trusted from any connection that ever carried a member HELLO.** Over the relay that is every relay sender, including strangers on a token-less relay. Each validator will dial up to 32 attacker-chosen public addresses with maintainers that retry forever, and the 32-slot lifetime budget never evicts, so the honest mesh is poisoned until restart. |
| **High** | CI-1 | **`main` has failed its scheduled release job on every run since October 27**, because the fix for the runner's `file(1)` wording change landed only on `m5-plan`. The scheduled-failure reporter watches govulncheck only, so nobody was told. |

Counts of enumerated findings: **Critical 0, High 5, Medium 13, Low 21, Info 6** (several Low and Info entries bundle related items).

A calibration note. M5 is the first milestone where coins buy consensus power, so "valueless testnet" no longer softens staking findings: the rules that decide who signs blocks are the product. Severity here is rated against what the README and deploy recipe tell an operator today.

## 2. High findings

### ST-1 · High · Partial unbond keeps a sub-minimum bond as a candidate; the cap turns one spark into a full seat

**Where:** `internal/state/stake.go:306-340` (`applyUnbond` checks only `tx.Amount > bond.Amount` and writes back any remainder), `:562-567` (`Committee` seats every bond with `Amount > 0`), `:613-640` (`applyPowerCap` clamps to a fixed point), `cmd/b10coin/main.go:666-705` (`unstake` refuses nothing below the floor), pinned behaviour at `internal/state/committee_test.go:388-421`.

**What the code does.** `applyBond` enforces `MinStake` on the resulting bond. `applyUnbond` has no floor at all, so a validator can unbond down to one spark and remain a candidate. The cap then iterates `p ← min(p, T/4)` to a fixed point, which pulls a dominant seat down to roughly the sum of the others divided by three. The repository's own test pins that 10^12 sparks plus three one-spark seats converge to four seats of power 1.

**Attack.** Hold one `MIN_STAKE` (1,000 b10). Bond it, unbond all but one spark (free), wait two epochs, withdraw (free), transfer the 999.99999999 b10 to a fresh key, repeat. Each cycle leaves a permanent one-spark candidate. With one honest seat at any stake `H` and `k` attacker seats at one spark, honest power converges to at most `floor(k/3)`: three attacker seats hold three quarters of the vote. On a committee-file chain (`CommitteeSize == len(genesis)`, genesis seats at power 1) the one-spark seats tie with genesis seats and the tie breaks on raw key bytes, which the attacker grinds. Cost: one `MIN_STAKE` and `2k` epochs of waiting. Nothing is spent.

**Fix.** Make the candidacy floor a state invariant: `applyUnbond` accepts a remainder of exactly zero or at least `MinStake`, with a carve-out so a bond already below the floor (a genesis seat) may exit only in full. Mirror the rule in the CLI. Add the inverse of `TestBondBelowMinStakeIsRefused` for unbond. This fix does not remove ST-2 below.

### RT-1 · High · Rotation-in is unimplemented at the node entry point

**Where:** `internal/devnet/tcpnode.go:368-375, 409-411` (`StartValidator` requires `SeatOfPubKey(g.Validators, pub) >= 0`, where `g.Validators` is the static genesis list), `cmd/b10coin/main.go:1060-1063, 1178` (same check in the CLI), `internal/consensus/driver.go:166-175` (the consensus layer does admit a key seated at head+1 that is not in genesis).

**What the code does.** The README and plan promise that `b10coin stake` buys a seat "considered at the next epoch boundary". The consensus layer supports it. No shipped binary can start a node for such a key: the refusal says the key "is not listed in the committee's genesis".

**Scenario.** Operator D bonds 1,000 b10 and is seated at the next boundary, evicting a genesis seat (RT-2). D runs `b10coin node --genesis committee.json --key d.key` and is refused. D's seat never votes. The quorum bar now includes a permanently silent seat; two such seats on a six-seat chain reach the halt point.

**Fix.** Check the key against `CommitteeAt(Height()+1)` ∪ genesis after the chain is opened, keep the genesis list as the driver's base config, and add an end-to-end test in which a sixth key bonds into a five-seat chain, starts a node after the boundary, and has its votes counted. Decide RT-3 (rotated-out non-genesis validators are frozen) together with this.

### RT-2 · High · Genesis seats at one spark versus `MIN_STAKE` at 10^11: committee capture is cheap on the shipped recipe

**Where:** `internal/chain/chain.go:146-152` (genesis bonds seeded with `Amount = Power`, unscaled), `internal/genesis/committee_file.go:146` (`CommitteeSize = len(vals)`, no spare seats), `scripts/deploy/committee.example.json` (`"power": 1`), `scripts/deploy/README.md:479-505` (funding arithmetic), `internal/state/stake.go:551-597`.

**Arithmetic, checked against `applyPowerCap`.** `N` genesis seats at one spark, attacker with `k` seats at 10^11, `CommitteeSize = N`, each attacker seat evicting one genesis seat. For `N = 6`: `k = 2` gives the attacker four of eight power units and a permanent halt; `k = 3` gives nine of twelve and the attacker commits alone. For `N = 3`, the Pi recipe, the cap is 1/1 below four seats, so `k = 1` holds 10^11 of 10^11 + 2, about 100%.

**Funding.** The deploy README argues that staking is unreachable because "1,000 claims take 1,000 epochs" with the CLI's ephemeral claim key. That is a property of the shipped CLI, not the protocol: claims are one b10 per key per epoch, eight per block, transfers exist, and the faucet is refilled by emission at 0.5 b10 per block. About 1,001 keys and a 50-line client accumulate 1,000 b10 within roughly 125 blocks of claiming once the faucet holds it, which emission supplies after about 2,000 blocks, or just over an hour at two-second blocks. The README's "23 days" is wrong by two orders of magnitude.

**Fix.** Refuse `power < MinStakeSparks` in `ParseCommitteeJSON` (or scale `power` to b10 and require at least 1,000) and update the example file; let the committee file set a `committee_size` above `len(validators)` so admission does not imply eviction; correct the funding arithmetic; document that the 1/4 cap is per key, so `k` keys buy `k/N` of the committee.

### PX-1 · High · Peer-exchange hints are unsigned and any relay sender is a hint authority

**Where:** `internal/wire/peerexchange.go:79-81` (`PeerExchange{Addrs []string}`, no signature), `internal/devnet/peerexchange.go:198-207` (`attestedConnection`: true if any member's HELLO arrived on this connection within four waves), `:228-280` (`observePeerExchange` calls `peerAdd` for every accepted hint), `:68-86` (budgets: 8 new dials per wave, 32 lifetime, no eviction), `internal/transport/tcp/tcp.go:842-856` (a maintainer lives until `Close` and redials forever), `internal/relay/peerexchange_forward_test.go:28-47` (the relay forwards the frame unchanged to every connection).

**What the code does.** The design argument was that the connection identifies the sender. On the relay it does not: every member's HELLO arrives under the single `relay:<addr>` name, so the relay connection is "attested" by any live committee, and the actual sender of a hint is never identified. The recipe explicitly supports a token-less relay (allowlist only), and on a gated relay any token holder or passive observer who captured the plaintext token is a sender.

**Consequences.** (1) Dial amplification against third parties: one frame of eight public addresses makes every validator open a maintainer per address, each redialling forever with a 30-second backoff cap, from the validators' real public IPs. (2) Mesh denial: four waves fill the 32-slot lifetime budget with junk that never evicts; the honest mesh cannot form until every node restarts. (3) Loss of the relay token as a boundary: a hinted listener that answers the two-frame ID handshake is admitted and receives every broadcast without ever holding the token (PX-2).

**Why the tests miss it.** The stranger test uses a direct connection with no `via` entry; the relay test uses only honest members.

**Fix.** Sign `PeerExchange` with the advertising member's key over chain ID, a wave or height, and the address list, and require the key to be a committee member at that height. Alternatively accept hints only from direct, HELLO-admitted connections and never from `relay:` names. In either case evict learned addresses whose handshake never completes and make the per-wave budget per source.

### CI-1 · High · `main` has failed every scheduled release since October 27, and nothing reports it

**Where:** `scripts/build-release.sh` (fixed by `26e6857`, which `git branch -r --contains` places only on `origin/m5-plan` and `origin/ci-release-arm64-token`); PR 16 was opened against `m5-plan`, not `main`; scheduled runs on `main` at `1e35483` on 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-30 fail the `release` job; `scripts/ci/file-toolchain-issue.sh` fires only on a govulncheck failure.

**What happened.** The `ubuntu-latest` image moved and `file(1)` now prints `ARM64` or `Aarch64`; the script matched the literal `aarch64`. The fix is correct (format token still required, architecture matched as a case-insensitive alternation) but never reached the branch the schedule runs. Any release built from `main` today fails. This is exactly the "red cron nobody sees" case the reporter script was written for, and it is blind to it.

**Fix.** Cherry-pick `26e6857` to `main` through a PR. Extend the scheduled-failure reporter to any failed job on a `schedule` event, not just govulncheck.

## 4. Low and informational findings

### 4.1 Staking

- **ST-5 · Low** Mempool admits `Bond`, `Unbond` and `Withdraw` with no stateful check beyond the nonce floor: a fresh key with no balance or bond can hold 64 fee-free `Withdraw` transactions; 16 keys fill a 1,000-slot pool for one block, refillable each block (`mempool.go:271-311`). Add the cheap map lookups `admitClaim` already does.
- **ST-6 · Low** `genesis.Validate` accepts `MinStakeSparks == 0` and `UnbondingEpochs == 0`; such a genesis opens and every staking transaction then fails `ErrStakeUnconfigured`, so a hand-edited genesis silently has no rotation (`genesis.go:172-330`). Refuse zero as `MinFeeSparks` is refused.
- **ST-7 · Low** No slashing of any kind, and an unbonded validator keeps its memoised seat and voting duty to the epoch end with zero stake behind it. The plan states slashing is out of scope; README's staking section and `SECURITY.md` do not (`stake.go:281-349`, plan `:894`).
- **ST-8 · Low** No golden vector for the bond or unbonding leaf encodings; every stake-root test is differential, so swapping `Amount` and `Height` in a leaf passes the suite (`state.go:258-287`, `stake_test.go:450-520`).
- **ST-9 · Low** Missing failure-path pins: no staking rows in the no-partial-write table (`apply_test.go:180-420`), no test for the merge-overflow guard at `stake.go:326`, no root-unchanged assertions for `ErrUnbondExceedsBond`, `ErrBadNonce`, `ErrNothingToWithdraw`, nothing pins the sub-floor unbond either way, nothing unbonds at `E−1` and `E` and checks the two release heights.
- **ST-10 · Low** `/account` is unpaginated and clones plus sorts the unbonding list per request on an unauthenticated endpoint outside the `/tx` limiter; bounded only by ST-4 (`server.go:255-268`).
- **ST-11 · Info** Plan-versus-code drifts that are documented and correct: the bond fee is burned (plan said "sum unchanged"); cap passes are 256, not 64, with a derivation showing 64 halts a reachable committee. `ParseB10` and `FormatB10` are correct and well tested.

### 4.2 Consensus and rotation

- **RT-5 · Low** The replay-before-bucket reorder lets an authenticated member flood the 4,096-entry replay set with distinct nonces at no token cost (each costing the server a verify), evicting other members' served nonces and re-enabling the replay drain the reorder closed (`sync.go:623-656, 859-864`). Check the replay set before charging, and record only after the bucket admits.
- **RT-6 · Low** `Chain.CommitteeAt` deep-copies the committee (up to 255 key allocations) on the router's membership path for every vote and proposal; `syncSeatsAt` builds an O(N²) union per sync message (`chain.go:327-331`, `router.go:177-189`, `sync.go:458-483`). Add a non-copying `IsSeatedAt`.
- **RT-7 · Low** Any genesis key is admitted and may spend sync budget forever, even after unbonding and withdrawing epochs ago, and any key can authorise a pull by naming a height in an epoch it once held (`tcpnode.go:867-881`, `sync.go:844-849`). Acknowledged as authorisation-only.
- **RT-8 · Low** The proposer of the deciding block `e·E−1` chooses which bonds and unbonds decide epoch `e`'s seats, and ties among equal stakes break on raw key bytes, which are grindable (`chain.go:590-603`, `stake.go:520-521`). Document both.
- **RT-9 · Low** `genesis.Validate` does not require `CommitteeSize >= len(Validators)`; a crafted genesis seats a top-N subset while `StartValidator` admits every listed key (`genesis.go:319-321`). Unreachable through the committee-file loader.
- **RT-10 · Info** An unseated engine that receives round 0's proposal still enters round 1 and can persist a lock for a height it has no seat at; harmless, but the "emits nothing, enters no round" comment is only true without a proposal (`engine.go:412-428, 995-1052`).
- **RT-11 · Info** The timeout ladder is uncapped again by design (`98bc291`), with the cost stated in a long comment: an undecided height's round cost grows linearly without bound, and the jump gate stays deaf to a single member. Rotation starts a fresh ladder per epoch.

### 4.3 Networking

- **PX-5 · Low** A hint-dialled listener announcing a predictable validator ID (`v1`) evicts the honest direct link at the smaller-ID end through `newcomerWins`; the relay link survives, so this degrades the mesh rather than partitioning (`tcp.go:1178-1270`, `tcpnode.go:470`).
- **PX-6 · Low** A hint naming the relay's own `IP:port` is not reserved (only the `relay:` prefix is) and the outbound registry is keyed by the literal string, so the node opens a second, direct-mode connection to the relay: doubled traffic on a token-less relay, a perpetual `unauthorized` redial on a gated one (`peerexchange.go:118, 148`, `tcp.go:832`).
- **PX-7 · Low** The local-range filter misses CGNAT `100.64/10`, most of `0/8`, `192.0.0.0/24`, `198.18/15`, `240/4`, the broadcast address, IPv6 site-local and NAT64-embedded loopback; IPv6 zone suffixes make one target consume several budget slots (`peerexchange.go:148`).
- **PX-8 · Low** Port-forwarded home nodes can never be advertised: `advertisableAddr` returns nothing unless the bound address itself is a concrete public IP, so `--listen 0.0.0.0:7001` silently advertises nothing, while the deploy README says a forwarded port is enough (`peerexchange.go:176-190`, `scripts/deploy/README.md:513-518`). Add `--advertise ADDR`; log once when a listener exists but nothing is advertisable.
- **PX-9 · Info** The token frame is still read under the 1 MiB frame bound (`relay.go:1092`); the greeting defends against misconfiguration only, never against an attacker who controls the dialled endpoint, which the docs should say; HELLO admission now unions genesis, the committee at the announced height and the committee at head, so a rotated-out genesis seat keeps admission forever (acknowledged).

### 4.4 CLI, RPC, tests and docs

## Appendix: verified-sound properties

- Committee derivation reads only `params` and `bonds`, sorts candidates under a total order (amount descending, key bytes, address), applies the cap after selection, checks every power sum, and errors rather than guessing when it cannot converge or has no candidates (`stake.go:513-660`).
- Each epoch's committee is memoised exactly once from the state after block `e·E−1`, in both `Append` and replay, and epoch 0 from the genesis state at `Open`; a miss is an error, never a head derivation; pinned by `TestTheBoundaryCommitteeSurvivesReplay` (`chain.go:208-211, 573, 772`).
- Driver config, router membership (before `Verify`), tally `IndexOf`, certificate verification on serve, record and adopt, and HELLO and sync authorisation all take the committee for the block's own height from the chain; a Byzantine sync server cannot substitute a committee because the puller's set comes from its own committed state.
- The cap fixed point satisfies `4p ≤ T`, is bounded at 256 passes with a derivation of the worst case, cannot produce a zero-power seat at four or more seats, and falls back to 1/1 below four seats on both sides of the package boundary; a genesis that passes `Validate` is unchanged by the clamp.
- Lock and round records are restored into the new-epoch engine untouched by rotation; a boundary whose committee cannot be derived halts the driver rather than falling back to the old set.
- Bond, unbond and withdraw run every validation before the first write, including the merge-overflow pre-scan; `Amount + Fee`, bond accumulation, record merge, matured sum, balance credit, `UnbondingEpochs × EpochBlocks` and `height + cooldown` are all overflow-checked; unbonding coins are in neither the account nor the bond map; release is at exactly `h + cooldown` with one payout per record; an unbond in epoch `e` releases no earlier than `(e+2)·E`.
- All three staking types are chain-bound through the genesis hash, authority-bound through `From == AddressFromPub(PubKey)`, nonce-consuming, and covered by the mempool's pending-nonce set; the state root commits bonds and unbonding under distinct domains with sorted keys and no map iteration, and a state with no stake records hashes exactly as before M5.
- `DecodeTx` accepts exactly tags 1 to 5 with fixed-width bodies and `Done()`; `DecodePeerExchange` bounds count and entry length before allocation, refuses empty entries and non-minimal varints, and has a fuzz target; tag `0x83` is above the wire floor and pinned in both directions.
- The relay still decodes nothing (pinned structurally by an AST scan), compares the token in constant time, enforces a 16-byte minimum on both sides, releases the handshake slot on every failure path, and refuses the `relay:` name on every direct connection.
- Peer hints are literal `IP:port` only (no resolver on the consensus path), refuse unspecified, multicast, self and (by default) loopback, private and link-local, cost no budget when duplicated, and never dial inline on the dispatch goroutine; only the node's own single address is ever advertised, once per four waves.
- The staking verbs read the key only through `keystore.Load`, sign over the node-reported genesis hash, derive and check `From`, print no key material, write nothing to disk, and use fee 0 for exits exactly as consensus requires; `ParseB10` is integer-only with every overflow checked.
- `/account` reads an immutable snapshot, holds no lock across encoding, echoes the address canonically and leaks no paths; `/status` is additive.
- The Nakamoto series is computed from memoised per-height committees with strict `> 1/3` in integers, no floats, no state change, offline only.
- Store files self-identify with magic and version; an unrecognised format is refused with a "do not truncate" hint and bytes untouched.
- No new `panic` reachable from the network, no new `os.RemoveAll`, no new `time.Now()` in consensus, state or chain paths, no secret in any new log line; `math/rand` appears only in seeded tests.
